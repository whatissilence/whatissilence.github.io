The Ukrainian Institute of Yoga and Yoga Therapy (UIYT) was founded in 2013

It is the first and only non-governmental organization in Ukraine dedicated to scientific research in the field of Yoga Therapy

Goals

  • to promote Yoga as a system aimed at human development and the realization of human potential, and Yoga Therapy as a system focused on improving and maintaining human health;
  • to implement and actively support scientific research in the fields of Yoga and Yoga Therapy;
  • to promote greater health awareness among individuals and the Ukrainian nation, and to encourage the spread of healthy lifestyle principles among individuals and society as a whole;
  • to advocate for a healthy lifestyle as a promising direction for the development of individuals and the Ukrainian nation.

Areas of Activity

  • Education and Public Outreach

    • organizing workshops, seminars, and public lectures

    • implementing health-promoting, cultural, and educational activities in Yoga, Yoga Therapy, and related fields

  • Training

    • teaching Yoga and Yoga Therapy in group and individual sessions

    • training professionals in the fields of sports and health

  • Research

    • implementing and actively supporting the planning and conduct of scientific research in the fields of Yoga and Yoga Therapy

    • promoting a scientific approach to Yoga Therapy and other health-promoting practices
